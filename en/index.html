About us

Young people who volunteered to build a more cohesive and fairer society

The organization began in 2013 as a volunteer youth initiative, driven by a belief in the role of humanitarian and development work in building society, and was later officially registered as a civil society organization.

We support families in need through diverse humanitarian and development projects, and promote a culture of volunteering and cooperation among Iraqi youth.
